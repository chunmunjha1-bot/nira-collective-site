About

Formed in New Jersey in 2026, The NiRa Collective LLC exists for a simple reason: good businesses deserve a steady home. We hold and grow companies across publishing, social media marketing, and e-commerce — giving each one room to operate while carrying the financial and legal weight at the parent level, so founders and teams can focus on the work itself.
